Kiro 98 Solitaire
Build the four piles at the top up by suit, Ace to King. In the seven columns, stack cards down in alternating colors; only a King can fill an empty column. Click the deck to deal. Drag cards, or click a card and then click where it goes. Double-click a card to move it automatically: to its suit pile if it fits, otherwise onto a column that takes it. Draw 3 deals three at a time (harder). Undo any time.
